About Us

Three Brothers.
One Unified Vision.

The SAY Group was born from a simple but powerful idea: that three brothers — Salman, Asad, and Yasir — could each build a world-class enterprise in their own domain, and together form a unified powerhouse of manufacturing and export.

From the precision of surgical instruments to the reach of global commodity trading and the energy of sports apparel, the SAY Group combines expertise across vastly different industries into a single, synergistic family brand rooted in Pakistan and serving the world.
